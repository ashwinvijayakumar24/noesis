---

## Context

The product is live at **https://noesis.is** and fully production-ready. Ashwin has completed all technical work for the month — the only job now is getting real researchers using it.

Noesis is an AI research intelligence tool that critiques research drafts **before peer review**. It identifies unsupported claims, coverage gaps, and missing citations from the researcher's own literature. It does **not** rewrite anything — it behaves like an expert academic reviewer.

### Task 13 — 10 User Success Calls (ongoing from Week 2)

Schedule 30-minute calls with your most active users (anyone who has analyzed 2+ drafts).

**Questions to ask:**
1. What problem were you trying to solve when you found Noesis?
2. Did it help? What specifically was useful?
3. What's missing or frustrating?
4. Would you pay for this? How much?
5. Pro at $12/month vs Team plan for your lab — which fits better?
6. Would your PI / department / university pay for this?
7. Would you recommend it to colleagues? Have you already?

**Document every response.** These calls directly inform when we activate Stripe and what to prioritize next.
