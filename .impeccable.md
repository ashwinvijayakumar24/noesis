## Design Context

### Users
Noesis is for serious academic researchers, starting with Georgia Tech researchers and expanding to universities. Users bring their own manuscripts and literature collections to evaluate claims, coverage gaps, citation support, and defensibility before submission. The interface should support focused, high-trust research work rather than casual exploration or AI-writing assistance.

### Brand Personality
Precise, credible, and restrained. The product should feel like an expert peer reviewer or research mentor: sharp, calm, and trustworthy. Emotional goal: confidence without hype. The UI should signal rigor, not novelty theater.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark application UI anchored in Noesis's established design system: charcoal backgrounds, rose-crimson accent, subtle borders, restrained motion, and tight visual consistency. This should feel premium and academic, not consumer-social or sci-fi. Anti-directions: generic AI dark mode, neon/cyan or purple gradients, glassmorphism, oversized metrics, playful startup illustrations, loud rounded cards, or anything that implies the product writes the paper for the user.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve trust first. Every visual decision should reinforce seriousness, clarity, and transparency.
2. Stay within the established token system. `tailwind.config.js` is the source of truth for colors, borders, radii, typography, and transitions.
3. Keep interfaces dense enough for expert workflows, but never cluttered. Prioritize hierarchy, scanability, and fast comprehension.
4. Emphasize critique and evidence over spectacle. Avoid decorative patterns that make the product feel generic or overstated.
5. Maintain consistent UI discipline: `rounded-xl` maximum radius, `font-semibold` headings, 150ms transitions, Heroicons outline set, and Tailwind tokens instead of inline styling.
