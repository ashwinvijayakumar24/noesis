  "One Spot Left" - 60-90 Second Pitch                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                
  ---                                                                                                                                                           
  Setup Notes                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                
  - Setting: Clean background, good lighting, both looking at camera
  - Tone: Confident but authentic, conversational, energetic
  - Camera: Eye level, medium shot showing both of you
  - Delivery: Natural, like explaining to a friend, not reading

  ---
  Script (75-80 seconds)

  INTRO (0:00-0:08) - Co-founder 1

  [Look directly at camera, smile]

  "Hi, I'm [Name 1]—"

  Co-founder 2: "—and I'm [Name 2]. We're the team behind Noesis."

  Co-founder 1: "And here's why you should give us that last spot."

  ---
  THE PROBLEM (0:08-0:25) - Co-founder 2

  [Lean in slightly, more serious tone]

  "Every PhD student we talked to has the same nightmare: submitting a draft to their advisor or a journal, only to get it back covered in red ink saying
  'where's your citation?' or 'this claim isn't supported.'"

  Co-founder 1: "Months of revision cycles. Delayed graduations. Papers rejected."

  Co-founder 2: "And the tools they use? ChatGPT rewrites their work. Grammarly fixes grammar. But nothing catches the research gaps before their advisor does."

  ---
  THE SOLUTION (0:25-0:45) - Co-founder 1

  [More energetic, confident]

  "That's what Noesis does. We built an AI that acts like an expert peer reviewer—"

  Co-founder 2: "—analyzing their draft against their literature to find unsupported claims, missing citations, and coverage gaps."

  Co-founder 1: "We don't rewrite their work. We critique it. Like a reviewer would."

  [Optional: Quick demo - hold up phone/tablet showing the interface for 2 seconds]

  Co-founder 2: "Real feedback. Before submission."

  ---
  TRACTION (0:45-1:05) - Co-founder 2

  [Show validation]

  "We've already built this. Production-ready platform. Beta testers using it right now—PhD students, postdocs."

  Co-founder 1: "And they all asked the same question: 'Is my unpublished research safe?' So we implemented enterprise-grade security—"

  Co-founder 2: "—database isolation, zero AI training on their data, bank-level encryption."

  Co-founder 1: "The kind of security that lets universities say 'yes' to campus-wide licenses."

  ---
  WHY US + WHY CREATE X (1:05-1:15) - Both

  [Co-founder 1, earnest tone]

  "We're technical founders who can build and ship fast. But we need Create X to—"

  Co-founder 2: "—validate our go-to-market, connect with early university customers, and scale this to thousands of researchers."

  ---
  CLOSE (1:15-1:20) - Co-founder 1

  [Look directly at camera, confident smile]

  "Give us that last spot—"

  Co-founder 2: "—and we'll show you how Noesis becomes the research tool every PhD student needs before they submit."

  Both together: "Thanks for considering us."

  [Slight pause, then smile and nod]
