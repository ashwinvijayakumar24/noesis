## Addressing VC Concerns

### Concern: "Researchers don't write papers anymore"

**This is MISLEADING.** Here's why:

#### Publication Volume is INCREASING
- **2010:** 2.0M papers published
- **2022:** 3.3M papers published
- **Growth:** +65% in 12 years
- **Trend:** Accelerating, not declining

#### What IS Changing:

**1. TIME Allocation**
- Researchers have LESS time for writing (more admin burden)
- **Opportunity:** Tools that make writing FASTER are MORE valuable, not less

**2. WHO Publishes**
- Industry researchers publishing more (Google, Meta, OpenAI)
- **Opportunity:** Broader market beyond academia

**3. HOW Dissemination Works**
- Preprints (arXiv) before peer review
- **Opportunity:** Need feedback BEFORE submission

**4. Pressure is HIGHER**
- "Publish or perish" still exists
- Funding tied to publications
- **Opportunity:** Higher urgency = higher willingness to pay

#### The Real Insight:
**Researchers still write papers. They just need to do it FASTER under more time pressure.**

This makes Noesis MORE valuable, not less.

## Unit Economics Analysis

### Can This Be Profitable?

**Revenue Per User:**
- Free tier: $0
- Pro tier: $12/mo = $144/year
- Team tier: $20/user/mo = $240/user/year
- Enterprise: $10-20K/year per university (100-200 users) = $50-100/user/year

**Blended ARPU (Year 2):**
- 60% Pro: $144/year
- 30% Team: $240/year
- 10% Enterprise: $75/user/year
- **Weighted average:** $166/user/year = $13.80/month

**Cost Per User:**
- Infrastructure (Supabase, OpenAI): ~$2-5/month per active user
- Support (with automation): ~$1/month per user
- **Total:** ~$3-6/month per user

**Gross Margin:**
- Revenue: $13.80/month
- Cost: $4/month
- Margin: $9.80/month = **71% gross margin**

**Comparison:**
- SaaS benchmark: 70-80% gross margin
- Noesis: 63% (acceptable for AI-heavy product)
- Improvement path: Optimize OpenAI usage, batch processing, caching

## Risk Assessment

### Key Risks & Mitigations

**Risk 1: Low adoption (can't reach 100 users in Month 1)**
- **Probability:** Low (Georgia Tech provides warm leads)
- **Mitigation:** Focus on quality over quantity, get 10 power users first
- **Pivot trigger:** <50 signups in Month 1

**Risk 2: High churn (>40% monthly churn)**
- **Probability:** Medium (draft analysis quality must be excellent)
- **Mitigation:** Week 1 technical improvements, user interviews
- **Pivot trigger:** >40% churn after 3 months

**Risk 3: Low conversion to paid (< 5%)**
- **Probability:** Medium (pricing/positioning challenge)
- **Mitigation:** Enterprise focus, free tier limits
- **Pivot trigger:** <5% conversion in Month 3-4

**Risk 4: Competitor launches draft analysis**
- **Probability:** Low-Medium (Elicit could add this)
- **Mitigation:** Move fast, build brand, lock in users
- **Advantage:** 6-12 month head start

**Risk 5: Can't reach $50K MRR by Month 6**
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Mitigation:** Enterprise sales, team plans, usage-based pricing
- **Pivot trigger:** <$20K MRR in Month 6 → Consider grant pivot
