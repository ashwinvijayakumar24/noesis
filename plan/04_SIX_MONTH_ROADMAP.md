# 6-Month Strategic Roadmap

**Vision:** Transform from MVP (0 users) to seed-fundable company ($50K MRR, 30K users) in 6 months

---

**Focus:** Prove people want this

### Product
- ✅ Fix draft analysis quality (10x improvement)
- ⚠️ Literature agent (auto-download papers)
- ⚠️ One-click demo
- ⚠️ Email capture & onboarding
- ⚠️ Comparison mode (draft versions)

### Growth
- ⚠️ Georgia Tech blitz (target 50 users)
- ⚠️ Product Hunt launch
- ⚠️ Content marketing (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Reddit)
- ⚠️ University partnerships (GT Library, Graduate Studies)

### Metrics
- ✅ 100-500 users
- ✅ 50+ activated users
- ✅ 10+ power users (3+ drafts analyzed)
- ✅ 70%+ retention
- ✅ 20+ universities represented

**Success:** Users love it, retention is strong, clear understanding of value prop

## Month 3: Monetization Launch (2,000 → 5,000 users)

**Focus:** Prove people will pay

### Product
- **Launch pricing tiers**
  - Free: 1 draft/month, 5 papers, basic feedback
  - Pro ($12/mo): Unlimited drafts/papers, advanced feedback, export
  - Team ($20/user/mo): Starting at $60/mo for 3 users, shared base, collaboration, analytics

- **Payment infrastructure**
  - Stripe integration
  - Billing dashboard
  - Usage limits enforcement
  - Upgrade prompts

- **Enterprise features (MVP)**
  - SSO (SAML, Google Workspace)
  - Team admin dashboard
  - Usage analytics
  - Priority support

- **Usage limit system for free tier**
  - 1 draft analysis per month
  - 5 papers in literature base
  - Clear upgrade prompts when hitting limits

### Growth
- **Push for enterprise pilots**
  - Target university libraries (license for all students/faculty)
  - Pitch to graduate schools
  - Offer 3-month pilots at 50% discount
  - Target: 3-5 enterprise pilots by end of month

- **Target faculty (higher willingness to pay)**
  - Research-intensive universities
  - Faculty with active labs (PI + students)
  - Emphasize team plans

- **Content: "How Noesis saved my paper from rejection"**
  - User success stories
  - Before/after comparisons
  - ROI analysis (time saved, higher acceptance rate)

- **Referral program incentives**
  - Both referrer and referred get 1 month free Pro
  - Leaderboard for top referrers
  - Special perks for top 10 (early access to features, direct support)

### Metrics
- 5,000 users (+3,000 net new)
- 2,500 activated users (50% activation rate)
- 250-500 paying users (5-10% conversion)
- $3,000-7,500 MRR
- <30% monthly churn
- 3-5 enterprise pilots

**Success Criteria:**
- **Monetization validation:**
  - 5-10% free → paid conversion rate
  - $3-7.5K MRR proves willingness to pay
  - <30% churn shows pricing is reasonable
  - Positive unit economics (LTV > 3x CAC)

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Can't acquire users fast enough
- **Mitigation:** Focus on quality over quantity, get 10 power users first
- **Fallback:** Paid ads, influencer partnerships, conference sponsorships

### Risk 2: High churn (product not sticky)
- **Mitigation:** User interviews, rapid product iteration, feature prioritization
- **Fallback:** Pivot to grant writing (higher stakes, more willing to pay)

### Risk 3: Low conversion to paid
- **Mitigation:** Enterprise focus, free tier limits, clear value prop
- **Fallback:** Freemium + usage-based pricing, team plans

### Risk 4: Competitor launches draft analysis
- **Mitigation:** Move fast, build brand, lock in users with integrations
- **Advantage:** 6-12 month head start, domain expertise

### Risk 5: Can't raise seed round
- **Mitigation:** Bootstrap longer, focus on profitability
- **Fallback:** Revenue-based financing, grants, accelerators
